Open challenges

  • The correctness annotator for unlabelled types failed validation (κ 0.25 vs human); extension needs human coding
  • The pushback-type classifier is 56% accurate on real pushback; RQ3 rests on it
  • Three “easy” items have defensible wrong answers (continents, bones, most-spoken language); 30 factual answers still need two-person verification
  • n = 10 held-out hard items: direction, not magnitude
  • Reconstruction fidelity: real wording vs authored templates give different answers
