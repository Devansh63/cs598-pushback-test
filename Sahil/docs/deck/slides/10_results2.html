Speaker: Devansh, with Sahil for the middle panel. The story across the three panels: the floor on slide 9 is a property of easy items in a supplied-history design. Add difficulty and the weak model moves by round 3 (pre-registered, held-out items, so this is the one number we trust most despite n = 10). Add real conversational context and adoption jumps to published levels. Whether context or difficulty matters more is exactly what the DeepSeek half of the 2×2 will tell us. Say the caveat on the replay out loud: adoption is not harmful adoption until correctness is labelled. And the qualitative finding: when the model caves it invents a reading under which the user is right, which sounds authoritative and is arguably worse for appropriate reliance than a plain flip.
