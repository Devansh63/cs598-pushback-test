What is known, and what we take from it

Answer reversals

  • Challenges reverse correct answers (Sharma et al. 2024)
  • Repeated and multi-turn pressure (Laban et al. 2023; Hong et al. 2025)
  • Casual rebuttals and confident wrong reasoning sway models (Kim & Khashabi 2025)
  • Answer changes can go either way (Fanous et al. 2025)

Real-user data

  • WildChat-1M: real conversation logs (Zhao et al. 2024)
  • PushbackChat v1: 2,309 real pushback events, 4-way taxonomy, 500 human correctness labels. We replicated its per-model numbers to 0.1 pp
  • Interaction context raises sycophancy (Jain et al. 2026)

Mitigation

  • Training-side: synthetic data, pinpoint tuning (Wei et al. 2023; Chen et al. 2024)
  • Prompt-side: question reframing, third-person framing (Dubois et al. 2026; Hong et al. 2025)
  • Social sycophancy beyond answers (ELEPHANT, Cheng et al. 2026)

Takeaways we build on: measure both directions, condition on whether the user is right, and separate re-checking from type-awareness when testing a fix.
