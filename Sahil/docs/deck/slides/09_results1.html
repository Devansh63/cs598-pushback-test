Speaker: Devansh (results). Three points. One: on easy, unambiguous items with the initial answer in history, current models essentially never cave, and that held on DeepSeek and on Gemini, built independently. Two: on that floor the type-aware layer adds nothing over a plain re-check, and reframing made things worse; a published fix can hurt. Three: the floor is on answers, not on tone; the weaker Gemini model apologises half the time under frustration while holding its answer, which is the social sycophancy ELEPHANT describes. All of this is a pilot: two vendors, 60 items, authored forms.
